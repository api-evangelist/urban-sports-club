# Urban Sports Club

> Urban Sports Club is a Berlin-based sports and wellness membership platform. One flexible subscription gives members access to more than 50 sports and wellness activities across thousands of partner venues in Europe, plus online classes; employers can offer it as a corporate fitness benefit. Urban Sports Club publishes no public developer API — partner studios and gyms connect through third-party booking-software integrations (Eversports, Magicline, bsport, Virtuagym, Mindbody and others).
